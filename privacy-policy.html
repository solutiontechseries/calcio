This policy explains how Calcio - Indian Daily Calculator handles information when you use the app. The app does not require an account to perform calculations.

Information stored on your device

The app stores calculation history and results, favorite and recently used calculator identifiers, saved presets, and preferences such as language, theme, number format, decimal places, and quick tools in local app storage on your device. Values you enter may contain financial or other information you choose to calculate. The app does not ask for your name, contacts, or location to perform calculations.

How information is used

Locally stored information is used to provide calculator history, favorites, recently used tools, presets, and settings. The current app does not send your calculation records or preferences to a developer-operated account or server. The current app build contains no advertising or analytics service.

Network access and app updates

Calculator operations run on your device and can be used offline. The Android app includes the Internet permission and uses Google Play’s in-app update service to check whether an update is available. Google Play handles that update check under its own terms and privacy information. This update feature does not send your calculation values to the app publisher.

Sharing, feedback, backups, and exports

When you choose to share a result, export history, create a JSON backup, or send feedback, the app opens an operating-system share interface, document picker, or email app. Shared results may contain the calculation title, inputs, and result. CSV exports contain saved calculation history. JSON backups may contain history, favorites, presets, recently used calculators, and selected preferences. A feedback email contains the message you choose to write and app version information. You choose whether to proceed and which receiving app or destination to use. The receiving service may process information under its own terms and privacy policy. Copies saved or sent outside the app must be managed or deleted by you.

Device authentication

If you enable App Lock, the app asks the operating system to authenticate you using security methods available on your device. The app receives the authentication result; it does not receive or store your device PIN, password, or biometric template.

Permissions and third-party services

Android declares Internet access for the Play Store update check. Importing a backup uses the system document picker, and sharing/exporting uses the system share interface. The app does not request contact, location, or camera access. The operating system, Google Play, email app, and any app or provider you choose to receive a shared file are separate services and handle information under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Your app data stays in local app storage until you clear it or remove the app. Use the app’s Data & Storage screen to clear history, favorites, recent items, presets, or all app data. Uninstalling generally removes local app data, though device backup features may retain a copy. Temporary export files may remain in the app cache until removed by the app or operating system. The app cannot delete copies that you have saved or shared to another destination.

Security

The app uses storage provided by the device platform. App Lock adds an authentication prompt but is not encryption and does not protect exported or backed-up files. Keep your device protected and use a trusted destination for files. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The app does not require an account or age information and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the publisher by email, contact us so we can address the request.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app or its information practices change. The app also provides privacy information in its Privacy screen. Please check the effective date above.

Privacy Policy

App: Calcio - Indian Daily Calculator
Publisher: Pankaj Kumar Prajapati
Effective date: October 20, 2026
Privacy contact: mr.pkp.dev@gmail.com

This policy describes what information Calcio - Indian Daily Calculator (“Calcio”, “we”, or “the app”) handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you. It applies to the app and its features described below. You do not need to create an account to use the calculators.

Information the app handles

Information you enter into a calculator can include amounts, rates, dates, labels, and other values. Depending on what you enter, these values may reveal financial or other sensitive details. If you save a calculation, the app stores its inputs and result in your device’s local app storage.

To provide app features, local storage may also contain your calculation history, favorite and recently used calculator identifiers, saved presets, and settings such as language, theme, number format, decimal places, and selected quick tools. App Lock settings and onboarding choices may also be stored on the device.

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, contacts, or location to perform calculations. We do not operate an account or calculator-data server for this app. The current app build does not include an advertising or analytics service, and we do not receive your locally stored calculation records or preferences through the app.

How the information is used

Calculator values are processed on your device to produce results. Information saved in local storage is used to show your history, favorites, recent calculators, presets, and preferences, and to support backup and export features when you choose to use them. We do not use this local information to build an advertising profile.

Network access and app updates

Calculator features work offline. The Android app declares Internet access and uses Google Play’s in-app update service to check for and install app updates. Google Play may process information such as app and device details needed to provide that service under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. This update check is separate from calculator processing; it does not send your calculation inputs, history, or preferences to the app publisher. The app does not use a developer-operated server for calculator data.

Sharing, feedback, backups, and exports

These actions happen only when you choose them:

  • Share a result: the shared content can include the calculator name, values you entered, and the result.
  • Export history: a CSV file can include saved calculation dates, calculator names, titles, inputs, and results.
  • Create a backup: a JSON file can include the categories you select, such as settings, calculation history, favorites, presets, and recently used calculators. The backup may include up to 500 history items.
  • Import a backup: the system document picker lets you select a file. Imported data is added to the app after you choose to restore it.
  • Send feedback: the app opens a sharing or email interface. Any message and app-version information included is visible to you before you choose whether and where to send it.

The app uses the operating system’s share interface, document picker, or email app for these actions. The receiving app or provider may collect or use information under its own terms and privacy policy. Review the destination before sharing. Files saved or sent outside Calcio are controlled by you and cannot be deleted by the app publisher.

Device authentication

If you enable App Lock, Calcio asks the operating system to show an authentication prompt using methods available on your device, which may include biometrics or device credentials. The app receives only whether authentication succeeded. It does not receive or store your PIN, password, or biometric template. Authentication is handled by your device’s operating system.

Permissions and third-party services

The app does not request contact, location, or camera access. App Lock uses the device authentication interface; backup import uses the system document picker; and sharing and export use the system share interface. Android Internet access is used for Google Play’s update service. The operating system, Google Play, email app, and any destination you choose are separate services. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies, which we do not control.

Storage, retention, and deletion

App information is stored in local app storage on your device and remains there until you delete it or remove the app. Use the app’s Data & Storage controls to clear history, favorites, recently used items, or presets, or to clear all app data. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data, but operating-system or device backup features may retain a copy. Temporary export files may remain in the app cache until removed by the app or operating system. The app publisher cannot delete copies you saved, exported, or shared to another destination; manage those copies with that destination.

Because the app does not maintain an account or a copy of your calculator records on a developer-operated server, deletion of local data is managed in the app or through your device. If you email us, we will receive the email address and message you send and use them to respond to your request. Please do not include sensitive calculation information unless it is necessary. Email copies may remain in your or your email provider’s systems.

Security

Calcio relies on storage and security features provided by your device and operating system. App Lock adds an authentication prompt; it is not encryption and does not protect exported or backed-up files. Protect your device, keep its operating system updated, and store backups only in destinations you trust. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The app does not require an account or ask for age information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the publisher by email, contact us using the address below so we can review the request.

Your choices and privacy requests

You choose what information to enter, export, or share. You can clear stored information through Data & Storage and disable App Lock in the app. You may contact us with questions about this policy or a privacy concern. Since calculation records are kept locally and are not linked to a publisher-held account, you control their deletion on your device.

Please review the policy periodically.
